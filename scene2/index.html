Mode A puts the self-calibrated broadcast camera into three.js — same focal length, same principal point, same pose — and draws the reconstruction straight onto the video. Nothing is fitted per frame: if the calibration or the 3D were wrong, the orange tube would drift off the shuttle. The HUD reports the distance between the projected 3D point and the tracker's 2D detection for the current frame.

Mode B is the same 3D scene seen from anywhere. The floor is the rally's own median background frame rectified through the ground homography, and each player is that frame's pixels cut out and stood up on the court at their measured foot position. It is a billboard, not a model — turn far enough and you are looking at a flat photograph edge-on.

Keys: A/B mode, Space play/pause, ←/→ step one frame.
